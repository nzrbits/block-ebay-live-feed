# Privacy Policy

Block eBay Live Feed does not collect, store or send any data.

It has no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. It makes no network requests.
It runs only on eBay pages. There it hides eBay Live elements and redirects
`/ebaylive` URLs to the eBay homepage, all inside your browser.

Questions: https://github.com/bonnbits/block-ebay-live-feed/issues
